❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TG BOT HUB? +
TG BOT HUB is a professional enterprise platform for managing Telegram bots. It allows you to add, configure, monitor, and scale multiple Telegram bots from a single dashboard without writing any code. Features include command management, analytics, AI integration, security, and more.
How do I add my Telegram bot? +
Simply get your bot token from @BotFather on Telegram, paste it into TG BOT HUB, and we'll handle the rest. Our system automatically verifies the token and connects to the Telegram API. You can then start configuring commands and features immediately.
Is my bot token secure? +
Absolutely. All bot tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. We use HTTPS for all API communications, JWT-based authentication, and never share your tokens with third parties. We also support 2FA and audit logging.
Can I manage multiple bots? +
Yes! TG BOT HUB is designed for multi-bot management. You can add unlimited bots (depending on your plan), group them by category, monitor their status in real-time, and manage all their commands from one unified dashboard.
What kind of analytics do you provide? +
We provide comprehensive analytics including command usage statistics, user engagement metrics, growth trends, traffic analytics, real-time activity monitoring, and custom reports. All data is visualized with beautiful, interactive charts.
Do you support AI features? +
Yes! Our platform includes AI-powered features including AI chat commands, auto-reply, content generation, and moderation. You can integrate with GPT models to create intelligent, conversational bots.
